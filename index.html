Cover Letter 01 — Thermal / Energy Systems Internship
Thư xin việc 01 — Thực tập Hệ thống Nhiệt / Năng lượng

Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to express my interest in the Mechanical Engineering Internship position focused on thermal and energy systems. I am currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering at VinUniversity, where I have developed a strong interest in heat transfer, battery thermal management, and simulation-driven engineering design. I am especially motivated by opportunities that apply mechanical engineering fundamentals to energy efficiency, sustainability, and real-world engineering systems.

My research experience has prepared me to contribute to a thermal or energy systems team. As a Research Assistant at the Center for Environmental Intelligence, I have worked on multiphysics simulation for battery energy storage systems, with a focus on coupling electrochemical heat generation and thermal transport. This experience strengthened my understanding of heat transfer, model setup, simulation interpretation, and the importance of validating engineering assumptions. In addition, my coursework in thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, heat transfer, and engineering design has given me a strong technical foundation for analyzing complex thermal systems.

Beyond research, I have gained practical experience with tools such as COMSOL Multiphysics, MATLAB, Simulink, SolidWorks, and ANSYS. These tools have helped me connect theoretical concepts with computational models and engineering decisions. I am comfortable learning new software workflows, documenting technical procedures, and communicating simulation results clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.

I am particularly interested in this internship because it would allow me to apply my background in thermal analysis and mechanical design to engineering problems with practical impact. I would be excited to contribute to projects involving energy storage, cooling systems, HVAC, battery packs, or sustainable energy technologies. I believe my combination of research experience, technical curiosity, and strong work ethic would allow me to make a meaningful contribution to your team.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to further discuss how my background and skills align with this internship.

Sincerely,
Nguyen Dinh Phong
